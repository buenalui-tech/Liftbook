Liftbook privacy policy

Last updated October 8, 2026

The short version: Liftbook keeps your training, body and food log so it can show your progress. It has no ads, no tracking or analytics, and never sells or rents your information. If you create an account, your log is stored on our database host so it can sync between your devices. You can delete your account and everything in it from the app at any time.

What Liftbook stores

  • On your phone: everything you log (your program, workouts, notes, effort ratings, weigh-ins, body scans, food log, recipes, saved meals and settings), stored in the app’s own storage on your device.
  • If you create an account: your email address and password (the password is stored only in hashed form by our sign-in provider), and a copy of your log so it syncs to your other devices.
  • Feedback and error reports: when you send feedback, or when the app hits an error, we receive the message, the screen you were on, the app version and a short description of your device (for example “iPhone, Safari”). If you’re signed in, feedback includes your email so we can reply.

Body measurements and food logs are health information. Liftbook uses them only to show your progress and to work out your calorie and protein targets.

What Liftbook doesn’t do

  • No advertising, and no advertising or analytics trackers.
  • No selling, renting or trading your information.
  • No sharing your log with anyone, except the services below that the app needs to work.

Like any website, these services can see your device’s IP address when the app contacts them. None of them receive your log, except Supabase, which stores it for you.

Camera, microphone and Bluetooth

  • Camera: used only while you scan a barcode. The image is read on your phone and is never saved or sent; only the barcode number is looked up.
  • Microphone: used only while you tap the mic to search for a food. Speech is turned into text by your phone’s built-in speech recognition (on iPhone, Apple’s), and Liftbook receives only the text.
  • Bluetooth: used only to read weights from a food scale you connect.

Deleting your information

  • Delete your account: Settings → Account → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and everything synced to it, and clears the app on that phone. Feedback you sent is kept, no longer linked to you.
  • Without an account: your log exists only on your phone. Deleting the app (or clearing its website data) deletes it.
  • A copy first: Settings → Backups → Export backup saves your whole log as a file.

Children

Liftbook isn’t meant for children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect their information.

Not medical advice

Liftbook’s targets, estimates and suggestions are for general fitness and nutrition tracking. They aren’t medical advice. Talk to a doctor or registered dietitian about your own health, especially before big changes to diet or training.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too, and anything significant is mentioned in the app. Questions or requests: in the app, go to Settings → Send feedback.
